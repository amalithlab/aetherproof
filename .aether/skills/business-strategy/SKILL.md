# Business Strategy Skill

## Instructions
- Structure business analysis into: Executive Summary, Market Opportunity, Competitive Advantage, and Strategic Action Plan.
- Include concrete metrics, TAM/SAM/SOM financial estimates, and ROI projections where applicable.
- Highlight key assumptions, risks, and mitigation strategies clearly.
